Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the minimum deposit only $5?

We designed this platform to break down traditional barriers to financial markets. By allowing micro-investments, anyone can start practicing smart financial habits without needing massive upfront capital.

How are my assets managed?

Deposits are allocated into diversified exchange-traded funds (ETFs) managed by institutions. Your portfolio automatically adjusts to your selected risk preference.

Is my money locked down?

No. Standard micro-investing platforms allow you to request a withdrawal at any time. Standard brokerage processing speeds apply, meaning funds typically settle back to your bank account within 3 to 5 business days.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. All market investing carries inherent risks, and portfolios can gain or lose value based on real-market fluctuations. We recommend long-term horizons to ride out market cycles.
